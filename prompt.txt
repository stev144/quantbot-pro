Calculate:

B. Cost-to-gross-profit ratio = total modeled costs / gross positive trading P&L × 100, when gross positive P&L is strictly positive.
C. Cost as a percentage of gross absolute trading P&L = total modeled costs / (gross positive P&L + absolute gross negative P&L) × 100, when the denominator is positive.
D. Cost as a percentage of traded notional = total modeled costs / total executed notional × 100, when executed notional is positive.
E. Profit retention ratio = net P&L / gross trading P&L × 100, only when the signed gross trading P&L is positive. Label this metric clearly and allow values below zero when costs exceed gross profits.

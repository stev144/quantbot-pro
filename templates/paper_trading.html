{# ── key finding note ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
Key finding & caveats exploratory · no deployment signal

Ranging gate impact is mixed: EUR/JPY shows a large improvement with the gate (+$9.01 gated vs −$0.76 free). GBP/JPY — the only VALIDATED pair — performs worse with the gate (−$8.91 vs +$17.41), suggesting the QC-1 strategy on GBP/JPY captures edge in all regimes, not just ranging. Applying the gate would reduce its edge by filtering out profitable trending-period entries.

What the gate does: New DCA cycles only open when ADX-14 ≤ 25 AND ATR ratio (14-bar / 50-bar) ≤ 1.7. Open positions run to completion regardless of subsequent regime change.

Governance (G5): The ranging gate was NOT pre-registered in QC-1's protocol. These results are exploratory — a post-hoc modification on the same data. A formal test would require pre-registering the gated strategy as a new protocol (QC-2) before seeing any results. Account currency: USD. GBP/JPY is the only validated pair (holdout consumed 2026-10-18).
